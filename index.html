Privacy Policy

Chime Blocks: Calm Puzzle

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short

  • Chime Blocks has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours. We do not ask for your name, your email address or any other personal details.
  • Your best score, your saved game and your settings stay on your phone.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To do this, Google's software inside the game uses your advertising ID, your IP address and some technical data, as explained below.
  • An optional one-time purchase, Premium, unlocks extra color themes and removes the ads between games. It is handled by Google Play: we never see your payment details, and the game only keeps a note on your phone that Premium is unlocked.
  • Chime Blocks is not designed for children under 13.

Who we are

Chime Blocks: Calm Puzzle (the "game") is an Android puzzle game made by Rajat Galar ("we", "us"). You can reach us at itechnowizard12@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The game saves a few things in its private storage on your phone so that it works the way you expect: your best score, the game you are playing right now (so that Continue works), your chosen color theme, whether you have unlocked Premium, your sound and vibration settings, and your privacy choices. We never receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads and Google

The game is free and is supported by ads. It uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show them, and Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it. You may see a short ad between games. When a game ends you can also choose to watch a video ad to keep playing; that is always optional. If you buy Premium (see below), the ads between games stop; the optional video stays.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google's software inside the game collects the following data and shares it with Google, to show ads, to measure how ads perform and to prevent fraud and abuse:

  • your IP address (Google may work out an approximate location, such as your country or city, from it)
  • identifiers: your Android advertising ID (if you have not deleted it) and other identifiers such as an app set ID
  • how you interact with ads and with the game, for example that an ad was shown or tapped
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

We do not receive this data and we cannot identify you from it. From AdMob we only see summarized reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned. We have added no other analytics or tracking tools to the game.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. To learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses data for advertising

The game asks Android for internet access (to load ads and to talk to Google Play), for the state of your network connection, for the advertising ID permission that Google's ads software uses, and for Google Play's billing permission (to make the Premium purchase). It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.

Premium (one-time purchase)

The game offers an optional one-time purchase called Premium. It unlocks extra color themes and removes the ads between games. There is no subscription.

The purchase is made through Google Play. Your payment details, your name and your Google account stay with Google: we never receive them. Google Play tells the game only whether Premium has been bought with your Google account. The game then keeps a note of this on your phone, so that the themes stay unlocked even without an internet connection. If you reinstall the game or use a new phone, the game asks Google Play again, so that you do not have to pay twice.

To talk to Google Play, the game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing component. According to Unity's documentation, the version used in this game does not collect personal data about app users. It may process some technical diagnostic and performance information temporarily, only so that the purchase works; Unity's documentation says that this information is not shared.

Purchases, receipts and refunds are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. You can see and manage your purchases in the Google Play app (Payments & subscriptions).

Your choices

  • Advertising ID and personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). You can also manage Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Consent message. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent message. You can review or change your choice at any time with the "Privacy settings" link on the game's home screen (the link only appears where the message applies).
  • Your purchase. Premium belongs to the Google account that bought it. To get it back on a new phone, sign in to Google Play with the same account and use "Restore purchase" in the game (the game also looks for it by itself when it starts).
  • Delete the game's data. Uninstall the game, or go to Settings > Apps > Chime Blocks > Storage and choose Clear data.

Children

Chime Blocks is not designed for or directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game, you can reset the advertising ID on that phone as described above, and you are welcome to write to us.

How long data is kept

We do not keep any data about you. Data on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google keeps ad-related data and purchase records for the periods set out in its own policies, linked above.

Security

Data that Google's software sends from the game is encrypted in transit (TLS). The data the game stores on your phone sits in the game's private storage, which Android protects from other apps.

Where data goes

Google may process data in the United States and in other countries where it operates. We run no server for the game and send nothing anywhere else.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, for example to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We do not hold personal data about you, so most of these requests can only be answered by Google. You can use the Google tools linked above, and you are welcome to write to us if you are unsure where to turn.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
